Privacy Policy

Tile Haven: Cozy Puzzle Match
App: com.duetrack.tilehaven · Developer: Monirujjaman Monjil — DueTrack
Effective date: 10 October 2026 · Applies to version 1.0 and later
In short: Tile Haven has no sign-up and never asks for your name, email, phone number, photos or any other personal information. Your game progress stays on your own device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which automatically receives some device information described below. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. We do not sell personal data.

1. Information you give us

  • None. The game has no account, login, chat or forms, and never asks you to type personal information.
  • If you email us yourself, we receive only what you choose to send and use it only to reply to you.

2. Information stored only on your device

  • Your game progress: levels reached, stars, coins, boosters, lives and their timers, win streak, daily-treat days, friends you have met, and settings such as sound, music and haptics.
  • Purchase bookkeeping: the date your Remove Ads subscription is paid up to, and the IDs of purchases already delivered (so an item is never granted twice). These come from Apple; they contain no payment details.
  • This data is not sent to us or to any server we run. It is included in your device's own backups (iCloud or computer) under Apple's terms. Deleting the app deletes it.

4. Advertising and your choices

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether Tile Haven may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised with it. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent: where the law requires it, a Google consent form appears first; your choice is respected and you can change it later.
  • Fair ads: no full-screen ad ever interrupts a level. Full-screen ads appear only after some wins, rewarded videos play only when you tap "Watch", and the small banner sits in its own space during play.
  • Remove Ads: the optional Remove Ads subscription turns off banner and full-screen ads while it is active.

5. Permissions

  • App Tracking Transparency (the tracking prompt above) — optional; the game plays the same either way.
  • The game does not ask for camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or notifications.

6. Children

  • Tile Haven is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Retention, deletion and your rights

  • We hold no personal data about players on any server, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. Deleting the app removes all game data from your device.
  • Data received by Google is kept and deleted under Google's policies; you can manage it at myaccount.google.com and reset your advertising identifier in iOS settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Contact us and we will help, including with requests to Google.

8. Security

  • Game data stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by iOS. Network traffic from the ad and store SDKs is encrypted.

9. Changes to this policy

  • If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
